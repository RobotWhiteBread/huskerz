Rewritten 2026-11-26 on the record as it stands after the Big Ten tournament: every number below is read from a file in data/reports/ (named in each section) as rebuilt that day, every rate carries its own denominator and interval there, and the caveats sit beside the claims. The first version of this page was written 2026-09-17 on 595 matches, after the independent statistics review of the five studies; each section keeps what that review changed, word for word, under its own heading. This page is for a reader who is not an analyst.

What moved since the first version. No verdict flipped. Three readings changed enough to say so: in section 1 a specification that leaves the score free now reaches p = 0.038, though nothing of that kind clears the cutoff shared over fourteen tests; in section 4 the 2026 season leader (Harper Murray, +7.9 sets) is "not established"; in section 5 the best opponent-adjusted hitting season is now Manaia Ogbechie's 2026, where it was Andi Jackson's 2025. The other numbers moved by small amounts and are given as they now stand.

What the record is. Nebraska volleyball, 2000 to 2026-11-25: 880 box scores (the 530 StatCrew pages of 2000-2015 came in on Sept. 17; before that the record began in 2016), of which 620 matches (1,911 sets, 86,174 rallies, 2001 to 2026) also have a rally-by-rally play-by-play that passed every check: 173 matches of 2016-2026 and 447 of 2001-2015. The 2000 season is side-out scoring, where a rally need not score, and is kept out of the rally studies on purpose. For the era study, the official season reports from 2000 to 2025: 341 player-season lines for 129 people. Nothing before 2000 has a per-player line in any file. Studies 1, 2 and 4 read only the rally record; study 3 rests on the official season reports, which end in 2025, so nothing since September has moved it; study 5 reads the box scores. Where a verdict differs on 2016-2026 alone, the section says so.

What the record cannot show, in every study below. A rally is one printed line, the action that ended it: a kill, an error, an ace, a block. No dig, pass, set or block touch that kept a rally going is recorded anywhere, so none of them is measured anywhere. Every study says so in its own file; it is repeated here because it is the single biggest limit on all five.

Every number above is read out of a file in this repository, and every file is re-derived and re-checked by huskerstats vb-validate, which wrote data/reports/coverage.json at commit 0fd141e. Hover a file to read its checks.
